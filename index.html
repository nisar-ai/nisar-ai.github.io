Configurable microservice simulator

Designed and implemented a discrete-event simulator to study how request arrivals, finite service capacity, queueing, simulated failures, and retries affect end-to-end latency.

  • Modelled a microservice pipeline with finite-capacity services and retries using exponential backoff with jitter.
  • Built seeded experiments and request- and service-level CSV trace exports for repeatable runs and detailed analysis.
  • Analysed mean and p50/p95/p99 latency, success rate, utilisation, queue wait, and bottlenecks.
  • Created an interactive interface and visualisations for exploring workload and capacity settings.
